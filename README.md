# HikeCatalyst Internship Challenge

Congratulations on being shortlisted. Pick the folder for the role you applied to inside `intern_challenge/`, and finish within **5 days**.

| Role | Folder |
|------|--------|
| Full Stack Web Development | `intern_challenge/full_stack_developer` |
| Social Media & Content Marketing | `intern_challenge/social_media_content_marketing` |
| Business Analyst / Development Associate | `intern_challenge/business_analyst` |
| IT Recruitment & Talent Acquisition | `intern_challenge/it_recruiter` |

Each role has a **medium** task (`medium.md`) and an **advanced** task (`advanced.md`). Do one. Medium shows you can do the job. Advanced shows you can be trusted with hard problems and is considered for the stronger offer track.

## How to submit

1. Fork or clone this repository.
2. Copy your role folder and rename it to your full name in snake_case, for example `john_doe`.
3. Add your work inside your copy.
4. Open a Pull Request.

## AI policy

**Use AI.** We expect it. We hire people who use AI well, not people who avoid it. These rules make that visible:

- Add an `AI_LOG.md`: the prompts you used, what the AI got wrong, and what you changed by hand. A log with no mistakes looks suspicious.
- Commit often. A single commit with everything in it will be questioned.
- Write your README, design notes and tradeoffs in your own words. We compare them with your call answers.
- Never paste real candidate or customer data into AI tools. Use made-up data.

## How we check the work is yours

- **Follow-up call (30 minutes, screen shared).** You explain one file or section, we change a requirement, and you adapt it live. AI help is fine on the call, but we judge how you steer it.
- **Traps.** Tasks include rules that prompts often miss (for example a grounding rule, messy data, or a deliberately buggy AI file to review).
- **Failure thinking.** Every task asks what breaks and what you did about it.
- **Honesty.** Saying "I did not finish X, and here is why" scores better than a hidden gap.

## Scoring (100 points)

| Area | Points |
|------|--------|
| Correctness and completeness | 30 |
| Handling of traps and edge cases | 20 |
| Understanding shown on the follow-up call | 25 |
| Judgement about AI output (log and review) | 15 |
| Communication and clarity | 10 |

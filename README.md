# HikeCatalyst Internship Challenge

Congratulations on being shortlisted. Pick the folder for the role you applied to inside `intern_challenge/`, and finish the tasks within **5 days**.

| Role | Folder |
|------|--------|
| Full Stack Web Development | `intern_challenge/full_stack_developer` |
| Social Media & Content Marketing | `intern_challenge/social_media_content_marketing` |
| Business Analyst / Development Associate | `intern_challenge/business_analyst` |
| IT Recruitment & Talent Acquisition | `intern_challenge/it_recruiter` |

## How to submit

1. Fork or clone this repository.
2. Copy your role folder and rename it to your full name in snake_case, for example `john_doe`.
3. Fill in the answers and add your work inside your copy.
4. Open a Pull Request.

## Ground rules

- **Use AI tools** (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Cursor, Copilot, and similar). We hire people who work well with AI.
- Be ready to explain every line, number or sentence you submit. We will ask you about it in the follow-up call.
- In every submission, add an `AI_LOG.md` listing the prompts you used, what the AI got wrong, and what you fixed by hand.
- Do not paste confidential or personal data into AI tools. Use the sample data we provide or data you make up.
- Commit regularly with clear messages. Late or partial work is fine if you tell us what is missing.

## What we look for

Correct work, clear thinking, honest notes on what you do not know, and good judgement about when AI output is wrong.

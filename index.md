# HikeCatalyst Hiring

Welcome. This site indexes our internship drives. Every drive has role-wise assignments. Each assignment is small enough to finish in about 5 days and hard enough that copying an AI answer will not be enough.

**Baseline for every drive:** use AI, log it in `AI_LOG.md`, build in your own public GitHub repo, and be ready to explain every line on a 30-minute follow-up call.

## How to apply

1. Read [how we score and our AI policy](README.md).
2. Pick the role and the tasks we assigned you.
3. Follow [the submission guide](SUBMISSION.md): your own public repo per task, then a pull request with your links.

> Partial work is welcome. Submit what you have, say what is missing, and explain why. An honest half-finished task beats a polished copy.
